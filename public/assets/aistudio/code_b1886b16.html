Newborn Screening Follow-Up Case Engine (v6.4b)

An auditable, zero-hallucination tracking suite designed to eliminate delayed recall in national infant screening programs without altering primary diagnostic registries.

Pillar 01alarm_off

The Day-108 Frontline Gap

In Indonesian primary care newborn screening for Congenital Hypothyroidism (SHK) and G6PD deficiency, positive or borderline lab results are safely stored in central databases yet often sit without an assigned health worker owner, concrete countdown deadline, or mandated notification audit.

Empirical Reality: A simulated G6PD field report in Cianjur demonstrated an infant was first contacted on Day 108 post-specimen collection—far beyond the critical therapeutic window.
Pillar 02lock_person

Thin Safety Layer Over National Systems

Designed as a non-invasive procedural overlay running above national registries (ASIK & SIRENE). Operates entirely using anonymous generated case tokens with strict client-side PII rejection—immediately discarding names, national identity numbers (NIK), and phone numbers.

Workflow Engine: Orchestrates 8 distinct operational statuses across SHK and G6PD workflows with locked clinical roles and verification timestamps.
Pillar 03psychology

Deterministic Zero-Hallucination Guardrails

The engine rejects black-box neural networks at runtime. It runs a mathematically locked rule engine (Frozen Rule Engine v5) underlying the v6.4b interface. Flags trigger automatically at 75% elapsed SLA time, escalating in red to the supervisory medical officer when overdue.

Clinical Defense Line: Abnormal clinical screening results can never be auto-dismissed without explicit cryptographic MD sign-off and Sundanese/Indonesian consent receipt.

Verification & Radical Honesty
